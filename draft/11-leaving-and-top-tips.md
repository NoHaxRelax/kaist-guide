## Leaving

### Departure checklist

In early December 2025 ISSS emailed every exchange student a departure guide, and on 17 December it sent a reminder, because many students still hadn't registered a bank account for their refund. These were the steps and dates in fall 2025. They change every year, so follow the emails ISSS sends you.

| When (fall 2025) | What |
|---|---|
| 1 to 12 December | Fill in the course evaluations, or you won't see your grades on time |
| Before you leave | Return your rented bedding to the dorm supervisor, then file the Campus Clearance Form on the KAIST portal |
| Move-out | Clean your room and submit the move-out checklist on KDS |
| Move-out | Register a Korean bank account for your deposit refund |
| Before you fly | Call NHI to settle your last payments and end your membership |
| At the airport | Hand in your residence card |
| By 5 January 2026 | Send the Personal Information Consent Form, which KAIST needs before it can issue your transcript |

Grades, the transcript and the credit transfer at DTU are covered in Academics.

### The airport run

Leave Daejeon in the morning if your flight is in the evening, which I learned the hard way.

My flight home left Incheon at 00:10 on 23 December 2025. I hadn't booked a bus ticket, and when I tried, the airport buses were sold out. My guess is that half of Daejeon was heading to Seoul to see family for Christmas. I did find a train, but I panicked that it wouldn't get me there in time, so I took an Uber all the way to Incheon. The ride took 2 hours 24 minutes and cost 213,100 KRW including tolls, about 1,000 DKK, so all in all it was a really bad trip.

For me, booking the airport bus online was "a labyrinth, like all Korean web pages". Go in person to the airport bus stop at Daejeon Government Complex, which is the stop KAIST recommends and about 5 to 10 minutes from campus by taxi, and buy your ticket one to two days ahead. The stops, times and fares are in Arrival and first weeks.

If you want to book online anyway, Bustago is probably the place. From what I've found since, the Daejeon airport buses are run by intercity bus companies, and a Korean booking guide sends you to Bustago (bustago.or.kr) for them. Its app has taken foreign cards since 31 July 2024 (see Travel). Kobus, the express bus site, only started taking foreign cards in August 2026 and covers express routes, so I wouldn't count on finding the airport bus there. I haven't tried either, so the ticket office is still the safe option.

The train is the backup (see Arrival and first weeks for the route), but trains sell out too, so book that ahead as well.

### Clearance form

Before you leave, you have to file the Campus Clearance Form on the KAIST portal (portal.kaist.ac.kr). In 2025 it sat under Academic, then Academic Affairs, then Others, then Student/Clearance Form Application. Return your rented bedding to the dorm supervisor first. ISSS warned that if you leave without filing the form, KAIST reports it to DTU and doesn't send your transcript.

### Dorm move-out

Clean your room and submit the move-out checklist on KDS, or part of your deposit can be kept. Under the dorm rules listed in 2026, leaving without the move-out checklist also costs 100 penalty points.

In 2025 the semester and the undergraduate dorm period both ended on 19 December, so check your move-out date against your flight. I left three days after the semester ended [ASK: dorm extension?].

Anything you leave in or near the dorm for more than 2 days gets a 7-day disposal notice, then goes into storage for 6 months before it's thrown out, and that includes bikes (rules listed in 2026). Sell your bike before you go (see Campus life).

### Deposit refund

The dorm deposit only comes back to a Korean bank account, which you register under MyDorm on KDS. In 2025 deposits were paid out on the 25th of the month if your checklist was done by the 20th, and refunds of the dorm fee for leaving early came on the 15th.

I never opened a Korean bank account. I got my deposit back through a Danish friend's Korean account, and he then sent the money on to me with MobilePay. It worked, but you're better off opening an account yourself or arranging this early. KAIST's order is residence card first, then bank account, and the bank on campus is Woori Bank in building W2.

### NHI and residence card

Before you leave Korea, call NHI to settle what you owe and end your membership. The English line was 033-811-2000 in 2025, and unpaid premiums can block visa matters later.

You hand your residence card in at the airport when you leave.
